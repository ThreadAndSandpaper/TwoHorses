When two horses like and trust each other, they can pull four times what each could alone. Emily and Jane are two horses that are friends.

Emily likes big picture stuff. She’s in charge of the narrative and characters. She is writing dialogue, art directing the places, and painting landscapes.

Jane likes fiddly little stuff. She’s in charge of the gameplay and making everything work. She is writing code, creating user interfaces, and painting plants and houses.

Wayward Trader

Caravans trundle through handpainted parallaxing landscapes as they move between fairy towns. Every town is populated by unique fey, from insectoid pixies, to talking animals, to strange speaking monoliths. Trade in strange goods—no wood or stone here, fairies buy and sell emotions and they pay with baby teeth. Wayward Trader lets you explore a strange, singular, and handcrafted world, all while you do something productive with the rest of your screen.
